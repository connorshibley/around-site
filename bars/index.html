How it's served

  • In the can, with a glass and ice. The table sees the country in bold, and nobody mistakes it for a soda.
  • On the cocktail list. One line per country, marked 0.0%.
  • Ready for your regulars. A bartender who knows the order can have it waiting, so no one has to ask for the non-alcoholic option out loud.

Built to a buyer's checklist

  • 0.0% ABV. Alcohol-free all the way, not "under 0.5%".
  • Cases of 12.
  • Shelf-stable. Made to sit on a shelf for at least nine months.
  • Three drinks to start. Mexico, Italy and Japan.

A bartender behind every recipe.

Each drink is made one on one with a bartender from its country of origin. They are credited on the can, paid for the recipe and take a share of the profit.

That gives your team a story to tell when they pour it: whose recipe it is and where it comes from. A recommendation with a story behind it is what gets a hesitant guest to try a first one.

How the partnerships work

Start with a 90-day trial.

  1. Taste it first. We follow up with pricing and send samples.
  2. Run it for 90 days. Put the drinks you choose on the menu, without committing fully.
  3. Look at the numbers. After 90 days you decide whether to keep ordering, change the mix, or drop it.

This is a demo request. Ar0und is a class concept, so there is no sales team. Nothing you choose here is saved or sent anywhere.
