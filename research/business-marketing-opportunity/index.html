Research/Unit 2

Business Marketing Opportunity

Type of Organization We Want to Sell to:

Causal Upcale Restaurants

Grocery Stores with Non-Alcoholic Sections

Why Our Product:

Its not just another mocktail, it's got real flavor built around a story. It fits right in on a cocktail menue and can be priced like one, without anyone having to spend hours developing a zero-proof recipe from scratch.

Grocery stores are interested because non-alcoholic cocktails are a fast growing drink right now, and next to the plain seltzer or basic mixers Ar0und stands out.

How is it Different:

A restaurant or grocery store is buying cases because their customers want it, they arent the ones drinking it. And its not an impulse buy on their end, they're going to taste it, compare to other options, and talk through pricing before committing to anything.

What Makes it a Business Market:

Way fewer buyers than individual customers, but each one orders a lot more at once. The demand only exists because it is tied to someone else wanting it further down the line. A restaurant doesn't want Ar0und for itself, it wants it because diners are asking for it. And once it's on the menu or the shelf, that's not a one time sale, it turns into an actual relationship you have to keep up.

What Kind of Purchase is it:

The first order is risky for them since they've never carried the brand before, so it would be a new buy. Once it's actually selling, reordering becomes easy and routine. If down the line they want to switch up flavors or order size, that's a modified rebuy.

What We Bring to the Table:

For a restaurant, it means no recipe testing and the same quality every single time.

For a grocery store, it's a product that's already proven to sell well in a category that's taking off, so there's a lot less guesswork involved.
