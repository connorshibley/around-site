Research/Unit 2

Consumer Influence

Below are the influences that matter most, what influences impact Carlos and how Ar0und plans to act

SOCIAL

Opinion Leaders

Factor: A trusted voice speaks well about product
Customer effect: Carlos trusts bartender's word and orders based on credentials

Brand response: Partner bartenders and creators, they share a story behind each recipe and the bartender would receive a royalty

Reference groups

Factor: His friend group sets the standard for what counts as a real drink on a night out
Customer effect: He's out with friends. He doesn't want fomo of having a juice when his friends are having cocktails. If he thinks he'll get called out, he might skip ordering or skip the night out.
Brand response: Partner restaurants serve Ar0und in the can with a glass and ice, so his friends see the country in bold, and clearly not a soda. The bold can is easy to share with #SipTheWorld.

PSYCHOLOGICAL

Attitudes

Factor: Has a bad opinion on NA drinks

Customer effect: Has had a previous bad experience will make it more difficult to try one

Brand response: Win him over by providing one at the bar through our trusted partners. Rely on our partners.

Self-Concept

Factor: How he sees himself and how he thinks others see him
Customer effect: He sees himself as someone who orders something good when he goes out and not just juice

Brand response: Pitch our drinks on taste first and alcohol-free second so it doesn't feel like a substitute

SITUATIONAL

Physical Surroundings

Factor: The setting where he buys, the bar, its menu
Customer effect: Bar treats NA as backup choice with limited options. He's confused by limited options looks for alternatives
Brand response: Partner restaurants carry Ar0und as if it was a cocktail and list it with the cocktails, one line per country, marked 0.0%, not with the sodas.

Social Surroundings

Factor: Who's around when he orders, especially a bartender who knows him
Customer effect: The bartender knows his order, so he doesn't have to ask for the non-alcoholic option or explain himself in front of his friends

Brand response: Partner bartenders get to know their regulars and have his Ar0und ready, so it shows up without him having to ask.

HIGH OR LOW INVOLVEMENT?

Low involvement, but not automatic: limited problem solving. Carlos knows NA drinks but doesn't know Ar0und yet. He's skeptical, he thinks many NA drinks are overpriced, and his friends see what he orders, so it isn't automatic. One drink at the bar on a bartender's word lowers the risk, and after some good experiences, ordering it becomes routine. Our job is to make the first one low risk and easy to access.

BUILDING ON UNIT 1 AND OUR STRATEGY

Unit 1 we planned to launch through our own online store first. Our Unit 2 we moved to the bar, where a trusted bartender does what an ad can't. They can give in person storytelling and customer relationship. The store, the points rewards club and #SipTheWorld are the repeat purchase.
