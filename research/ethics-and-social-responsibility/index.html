Ethics & Social Responsibilty

Cultural Responsibility

We understand there may be some ethical questions about Ar0und using recipies from around the world. We aim not to steal these recipies by any means but to simply make them accesable for all, regardless of whether or not they consume alcohol. Our team works one on one with bartenders from each drink's country of origin to create a completely authentic recipie. These partners are longterm relationships that we build and maintain throught the entire process and after. Partners recieve a percentage of profits on top of compenstation for the recipies and credit on the package. As for the cans themselves, we always, make sure the country of origin is on the cover in bold, to give credit where its due.

Environmental Responsiblity

As Ar0und does create authentic cultural drinks, sometimes the ingredients required to maintain that quality require outsourcing. This importation has an environmental impact that we believe is important to acknowledge. We are very commited to reducing our emmisions from shipping and manufacturing through instead building more sustainable direct trade relationships. Partners will be clearly posted on our site for complete transparancey. We work with distributors to choose the least impactful method of shipping and choose to use only recycled materials for our cans and boxes. While this does contribute to a higher price, we prioritize paying our partners fairly. Typically growers and distributors can see small percentages of the final profit, but we are comitted to changing that norm by prioritizing their cut first and foremost. While this is something we are proud of, we would also like to acknowledge that this effects the price that customers pay. The price reflects our decision to never cut corners or unfairly pay our partners and we hope that those goals are also important to our customers.

Why It Matters To Us

Ar0und is built on the love our team has for culture and food. We believe that culture should be shared and we hope to encourage people to learn and seek out more culture from the world.

Our Responsibility Beyond The Product

Our responsibility doesnt end at the drinks, it extends to all the people whose recipies and Ingredients helped make Ar0und possible. Our responsibility is also always to our customers to be transparent and ethical.
