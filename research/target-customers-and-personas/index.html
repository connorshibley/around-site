Target Customers and Personas

The Customer Persona

He doesn't want a mocktail. He wants an exciting drink that happens to be alcohol-free.

Carlos has expendable income and travels a few times a year, usually to cities where he can try street food and local drinks. He loves learning about new cultures and connecting on social media.

He cut back on alcohol to feel better in the mornings and stay on top of his health. He still loves going out with friends though and does not want to give up ordering something good while out.

✈️

🎉

What Carlos Wants:

  1. Well crafted, fully alcohol free mocktails that are not just juice.
  2. To reacreate flavors he tried while traveling or to try new flavors from around the world.
  3. Brands that are interactive, align with his morals and offer something new.

What hinders that in the current market:

The current availble NA drink options are often too sweet or too bland and many actually do conatin alcohol. Bars treat NA drinks as an afterthought causing carlos to feel left out and frustrated at the limited decisions.

Behaviors

Carlos scrolls Instagram and TikTok daily and saves recipe and drink videos to try later. He goes out with friends regularly and makes drinks at home on weekends. He shops online, reads reviews on Google and TikTok before trying new products, and shares things he likes on his own accounts.

Purchase Priorities

Taste comes first, especially drinks that aren’t overly sweet. After that he looks at ingredient quality, price compared to a regular cocktail, how easy it is to buy, and whether it looks good enough to post.

Barriers

He’s been let down by bland alcohol-free drinks before, so he’s skeptical of new ones. He also thinks many are overpriced for what they are and sometimes wonders why he shouldn’t just order a soda. If a product isn’t easy to find online or in stores near him, he’s unlikely to go looking for it.

Information sources

Instagram and TikTok creators, online reviews, and recommendations from friends he travels with. A recommendation from someone he trusts carries the most weight.

Carlos's Relationship with Ar0und

Carlos doesn’t know Ar0und yet, but he’ll most likely discover it through a creator video or a friend’s post. He’ll choose it over other options if the taste lives up to what’s promised and the brand gives him recipes and travel-inspired flavors. If that happens, he’s the kind of customer who buys again, joins the rewards club, and posts about it.
