Research/Unit 1

Competitive Strategy

Competitors: Seedlip, Ritual Zero Proof, Lyre's, BERO

SWOT SYNTHESIS:

Strengths to Overcome: 3 of 4 competitors are now owned by major beverage capital (Diageo owns 2 of these companies), we cannot compete with global distribution and budgets right now.

Weakness to Exploit: Not many companies own true alcohol free drinks or authentic global drinks. Lyre's is 0.5% ABV, not alcohol free, Ritual is American classics only.

Opportunity to Pursue: All four analyses found the same demand shift, people are moderating, not quitting. That is something we can persue.

Threat to Prepare for: The TTB's 0.5% labeling line plus a 50% aluminum tariff (starting in June 2025) and a separate import tariff raise real cost and compliance pressure.

BCG/ PRODUCT PORTFOLIO SYNTHESIS

what the portfolios revealed: Every competitor has a hero product, a couple steady sellers, and one that is a shot in the dark.

Where they're heavily invested: The bottled gin/wiskey/agave alternative

Crowded/Growing/Underserved: Crowded- Bottled American profile spirits; Growing but unclaimed- Canned ready-to-drink cocktails; Underserved- authentic global flavors

Where should we focus: Canned ready-to-drink cocktails built around authentic global flavors

PESTLE AND STRATEGIC RESPONSE

External factors that matter most:

Social- sober curious, moderation shift

Legal- The 0.5% ABV line separating "alcohol-free" from "non-alcoholic"

Greatest opportunity: convergent sober curious demand shift
