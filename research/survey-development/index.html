Research/Unit 3

Survey Development & Data Collection

Survey Objective was to find out whether adults 21-34 who drink once a week or less are more likely to order or buy a new non-alcoholic cocktail when a bartender recommends it than when they see it on a menu or a store shelf, and whether they go out often enough for a bartender-led business model to reach them.

Why We Chose Google Forms

Surveys are the fastest and cheapest way to collect primary data. Google Forms is free, it's anonymous, and every answer can be easily exported.

What Each Question Tells Us

Q1) Demographic. Screening. The goal was to focus on our target audience of ages 21-34. This is the data we are primarily focused on.

Q2) Geographic. Focuses on the region where our data is coming from.

Q3) Geographic. Focuses on whether our target lives where there are enough bars for a bartender-led model.

Q4) Behavioral. Looks at usage rates, from moderate drinkers to heavy drinkers to non-drinkers.

Q5) Behavioral. Looks at how often people go out, so we know if the bartender channel actually reaches them.

Q6) Hypothesis. Tests whether people are more likely to buy a new non-alcoholic cocktail when a bartender recommends it than when they see it on a menu, a store shelf, or a friend's post through social media

Q7) Psychographic. Agree/disagree statements on global flavors, how their drink looks, drinking less, and whether non-alcoholic drinks are too sweet, which shapes how we position Ar0und.

Q8) Behavioral. Where they would buy it again, so we know whether to add grocery and liquor stores after bars.

Who We Ask / How We Recruit / How We Share It

Who We Ask: Friends in our social circles

How We Recruit: Distributing survey link to as many HWS students as possible

How We Share It: Sharing via link and QR code shared live during our class presentation

How Responses Are Collected and Exported

Google Forms records every response with a chart for each question automatically. All answers also go into a linked Google Sheet, one row per person, which we filter to our target and download as Excel.

Limitations and Bias

  • 30 of our 51 responses were under 21, so we screened them out. Most of the rest are Northeast college students aged 21-24, one respondent was 35-44, and no one aged 25-34 has answered yet which is a big part of our target audience.
  • People say they'd buy things they never actually buy.
  • A small sample: only 15 people fit our exact target, so the results are pretty limited.

Next Step: Study in Partner Bars

If we had more time, we would run a study, watching customers in their natural setting instead of asking them. It fits Ar0und because ours is a social drink, and how people order and share drinks with friends is something a survey can't capture. A survey tells us what people say they would do but an observation shows what they actually do. We would partner with 2-3 bars in the Finger Lakes area, put Ar0und on the menu, and spend nights watching how guests order.

What We'd Watch For

  • Bartender vs. menu: How many guests order Ar0und after a bartender recommends it, compared with guests who just see it on the menu
  • Who orders it: Rough age, whether they're also drinking alcohol that night, and whether they're in a group or alone
  • When they order it: Early in the night, between alcoholic drinks, or as the last drink of the night
  • Group influence: Whether one person ordering it leads friends to order it too
  • Reactions: Whether guests post it, finish it, and order a second one
  • Questions guests ask: What they want to know

Observation would be the next step to confirm what our survey suggests

Privacy

All responses are anonymous we don't collect names or emails, so we don't know who took the survey. The data is for this class project only and will be deleted after the project

Our Answer

How will we collect useful information that helps answer our marketing research question?

We collect useful information with a short anonymous Google Forms survey that screens for our target, compares how likely they are to buy in each situation, and records every answer in a spreadsheet we can filter
